Metrics

Systems are ranked by execution-based Pass@1 on hidden tests, macro-averaged across language resource tiers T:

Score= 1|T| Σt ∈ T Pass@1(t)

Pass@1: the share of problems whose single generated solution passes all hidden tests. Each tier counts equally.
