Development phase · Oct 27, 2026 – Jan 7, 2027

  1. 1

    Register on CodaBench

    Create a CodaBench account and join the Task 1 competition. Each team submits from one account.

    Open now · Closes Jan 17, 2027

  2. 2

    Download the data and the scorer

    Trial and dev problems, the scorer, and the submission format. What's in it

    Out now

  3. 3

    Build your system

    Make it work across languages and language classes. Any public data, model or tool is allowed.

  4. 4

    Practice on CodaBench

    Generate solutions for the dev problems and submit them to see your dev Pass@1. CodaBench does not show why a file is rejected, so run the scorer's format check on your file first.

    Until Jan 7, 2027

Test phase · Jan 8 – Jan 24, 2027

  1. 5

    Get the test problems

    1,000 problems you have not seen, each with 2 of its 5 test cases shown. Run your final system on them.

    Released Jan 8, 2027

  2. 6

    Submit your final solutions

    Upload one solution per problem on CodaBench. Your score shows on the leaderboard; your latest finished submission is the one that is ranked.

    Deadline Jan 24, 2027, 11:59 PM AoE

After the competition

  1. 7

    See the results and write your paper

    The official ranking comes out on Feb 3, 2027. Then describe your system in a paper. Paper Submission

    Paper due Feb 15, 2027

Questions? Ask in the Forum of the CodaBench competition; the organizers answer within 24–48 hours.
