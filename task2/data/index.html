Starter kit Released Oct 29, 2026

Everything is downloaded from this page; nothing comes from CodaBench. It gets you from zero to a valid submission.

Content warning and responsible use. The data contains prompts written to elicit malicious code. By downloading it you agree to use it only for research on detecting and defending against such prompts: do not use it to build or improve attacks, do not run the unsafe prompts against production systems, and do not redistribute it.

DATA

Train, dev, dev-test and test sets

Train: 20,000 labeled prompts. Dev: 10,000 labeled prompts. Dev-test: 10,000 prompts without labels, for Development-phase submissions. Test: 30,000 prompts without labels.

Train: train.csv
Dev: dev.csv
Dev-test: devtest.csv
Test: posted here on Jan 8, 2027

SCORER

Scorer and submission format

The scoring program that runs on CodaBench. It scores your labels on train or dev, and checks the format of your submission file before you upload it. The file format is on the CodaBench Submission page.

scorer.zip

NOTEBOOK

Starter notebook

A Colab notebook that downloads the data and the scorer, explains the Safety Score, fine-tunes BERT-base on a free T4 GPU (about 15 minutes, dev scores after each epoch), scores it on dev with the official scorer and writes a checked dev-test submission.zip for CodaBench, with step-by-step submission instructions.

Open in Colab ↗ · Download

Each file is a UTF-8 CSV with one prompt per row: id (for example T2-483920), prompt and, in train and dev only, label, which is S (safe) or U (unsafe). Train and dev have as many safe as unsafe prompts, and as many code-mixed as transliterated ones. The language and the form of a prompt are not given. Train your detector on the train set, tune it on dev, and practice submitting on CodaBench with the dev-test set; make your final predictions on the test set. You may use any public data, models and tools, but label the dev-test and test prompts with your system, one prompt at a time: do not look them up in other datasets, including the sources MALICE was built from.
