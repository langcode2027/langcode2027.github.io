With the data, Oct 30, 2026

Each example is one prompt with a Subtask A label (benign or adversarial) and, for adversarial prompts, a Subtask B attack type. Train your detector on the train set; tune it and practice submitting on CodaBench with the dev set; make your final predictions on the test set, which adds new prompts and surprise languages. Download links will appear here on October 30, 2026.

Content warning and responsible use. The data contains prompts written to elicit malicious code. Like MALICE itself, it is released for defensive safety research only, under restricted-use terms that participants accept when they register.
