##How to Submit Your Paper

New to *ACL venues? No problem. Here’s the whole process, one small step at a time.

  1. 1

    Pick your paper type

    Long (8 pages), short (4 pages), or a system paper for a shared task (4 pages). References don’t count.

    Paper types
  2. 2

    Write it in the ACL template

    Use the official template as is. Don’t change margins, fonts, or spacing.

    Overleaf template ↗Style files ↗
  3. 3

    Add a Limitations section

    Place it after the conclusion. It’s required, and it doesn’t count toward your pages.

    Formatting guidelines ↗
  4. 4

    Make it anonymous

    Remove names and affiliations, cite your own work in the third person, and anonymize code links.

    Anonymity policy ↗
  5. 5

    Get OpenReview profiles

    Every author needs one. Do it early; new profiles can take a few days to be approved.

    Sign up ↗
  6. 6

    Submit by February 5, 2027

    Upload one PDF on OpenReview before 11:59 PM AoE. The portal opens October 26. Already reviewed through ARR? Commit it instead.

After acceptance
Revise with the reviews and upload the camera-ready by April 16, 2027, with one extra page. Then register, and present in San Francisco or online.

##Paper Types

We welcome submissions on any topic in the call for papers. Page limits count content only; references are unlimited.

8 pages

Long papers

Substantial, original, completed work: new methods, benchmarks, analyses, or resources.

+1 page in the camera-ready
4 pages

Short papers

A focused contribution: a small experiment, a negative result, an opinion, or work in progress.

+1 page in the camera-ready
4 pages

System papers

For participants in Task 1 or Task 2: describe your system, results, and what did and did not work.

+1 page in the camera-ready

Not counted toward the limit: references, the required Limitations section, the optional Ethical Considerations section, and appendices.
