##Paper Types

We welcome submissions on any topic in the call for papers. Page limits count content only; references are unlimited.

8 pages

Long papers

Substantial, original, completed work: new methods, benchmarks, analyses, or resources.

+1 page in the camera-ready
4 pages

Short papers

A focused contribution: a small experiment, a negative result, an opinion, or work in progress.

+1 page in the camera-ready
4 pages

System papers

For participants in Task 1 or Task 2: describe your system, results, and what did and did not work.

+1 page in the camera-ready

Not counted toward the limit: references, the required Limitations section, the optional Ethical Considerations section, and appendices.

##Format

  1. Use the official ACL LaTeX template, either the Overleaf template or the style files, unmodified and in review mode (with line numbers).
  2. Submit a single PDF on A4 paper. Papers that change the margins, font size, or spacing, or that exceed the page limit, may be desk-rejected.
  3. Include a section titled “Limitations” after the conclusion and before the references. It may not contain new experiments, figures, or analysis.
  4. Appendices go after the references. The paper must stand on its own: reviewers are not required to read them.
  5. Code and data may be submitted as supplementary material and must be anonymized.

Everything not covered here follows the ACL paper formatting guidelines.

##Anonymity & Review

Double-blind

Keep the submission anonymous

No author names, affiliations, or acknowledgments. Refer to your own prior work in the third person, and link code and data through anonymized repositories. See the ACL anonymity policy.

?:?
OpenReview

Submit and review on OpenReview

Every author needs an OpenReview profile before the deadline. Each paper is reviewed by members of our program committee.

##ARR Commitment

Papers already reviewed through ACL Rolling Review can be committed to LangCode with their existing reviews and meta-review, instead of being submitted for fresh review. Committed papers are not re-reviewed; the organizers decide based on the ARR reviews. The commitment deadline is March 12, 2027 (AoE); the commitment link is announced with the call for papers.

##Policies

Originality
Submissions must be original and unpublished. If a paper is under review elsewhere, say so when you submit, and withdraw it from LangCode if it is accepted at another venue.
Security research
Papers on attacks, jailbreaks, or malicious code should discuss dual use and responsible disclosure in an Ethical Considerations section, and should not include working exploits.
ACL policies
All submissions follow ACL’s publication ethics policy, including its AI writing guidelines, and all participants follow the anti-harassment policy.
