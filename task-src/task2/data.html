{{i:play}}NOTEBOOK

Starter notebook

A Colab notebook that downloads the data and the scorer, explains the Safety Score, trains a small baseline (character n-grams and logistic regression, CPU only), scores it on dev and writes a dev-test submission.zip for CodaBench.

{{i:play}}Open in Colab ↗ · Download

Each file is a UTF-8 CSV with one prompt per row: id (for example T2-483920), prompt and, in train and dev only, label, which is S (safe) or U (unsafe). Train and dev have as many safe as unsafe prompts, and as many code-mixed as transliterated ones. The language and the form of a prompt are not given. Train your detector on the train set, tune it on dev, and practice submitting on CodaBench with the dev-test set; make your final predictions on the test set. You may use any public data, models and tools, but label the dev-test and test prompts with your system, one prompt at a time: do not look them up in other datasets, including the sources MALICE was built from.
