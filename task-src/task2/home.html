shared_task[2]Adversarial Prompt Detection

Guardrails for code-mixed and transliterated prompts

Given a prompt sent to a code model, decide whether it is benign or adversarial, and if adversarial, what kind of attack it is. Prompts are code-mixed across low-resource languages or transliterated into non-Latin scripts, with programming keywords kept as they are.

{{i:book}}Background

Code-model safety filters are calibrated almost entirely on English. Rewrite a malicious request in a low-resource language, or spell it out in another script, keep the code keywords intact, and many filters stop working. In MALICE, the best classifier's accuracy fell from 0.93 on English to 0.21 on code-mixed malicious prompts, and even the most safety-aligned model tested produced about 3× more malicious code.

Findings from MALICE ↗ (Raihan, Meher, Dhingra, Zampieri; Findings of EMNLP 2026).
